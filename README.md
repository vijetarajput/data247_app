## 🌍 What is DATA247?

DATA247 is an AI powered mobile application for students and freshers in India who are preparing for Data Analyst, Business Analyst, Data Scientist, and ML Engineer roles. It solves a real and painful problem — interview preparation for data roles in India is fragmented, English-only, outdated, and disconnected from actual hiring trends.

No single app in India today combines Hinglish explanations, Business Analyst focus, Indian company targeting, and daily habit loops for data job preparation. DATA247 fills this gap.

---

## 😣 The Problem

Students and freshers in India currently struggle with:

- Interview preparation is English-only — Tier 2 and Tier 3 city students with strong technical skills cannot understand deeply
- Question banks are 2 to 3 years old and do not reflect current AI and ML hiring trends
- Apps focus on coding syntax but ignore business logic and decision making
- No platform targets Indian company specific preparation — TCS, Infosys, Wipro, GIFT City fintech roles
- No app combines Data Analyst, Business Analyst, Data Scientist, and ML Engineer preparation in one place

## ✅ My Solution — 7 Core Features

| Feature | What it does |
| :--- | :--- |
| 🌅 Morning Drill | 5 curated questions every morning at 7AM to build daily habit |
| 📝 MCQ Practice Bank | 450 questions across SQL, Excel, Python, Power BI, ML, AI, BA topics |
| 🗣️ Hinglish Explanations | Every answer explained in Hindi-English mix for deep clarity |
| 🃏 Flashcard System | 250 flip cards for concepts, formulas, and interview one-liners |
| 📊 Job Ready Quiz | 10 question diagnostic that calculates a Job Ready Score |
| 🏢 Company Packs | Curated question sets for TCS, Infosys, Wipro, GIFT City fintech |
| 📰 Daily Data News | Auto-fetched data and AI news every morning via RSS |
