## The Story Behind DATA247

**Founded:** April 2026
**Launched:** June 2026
**Location:** Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India
**Founder:** Vijetasingh Rajput

DATA247 started from a single observation:

> *Many learners in India have access to information — but not access to structured preparation.*

Structured data analytics preparation has largely been accessible to learners in tier-1 cities — those with access to premium coaching institutes, English-medium education, and high-cost online platforms.

However, a large and often overlooked segment of aspiring data professionals — learners from tier-2 cities, tier-3 cities, and rural India — face a very different reality:

- Quality preparation content is expensive and out of reach
- Most platforms assume full English fluency
- Coaching institutes charge fees that many cannot afford
- Structured learning paths are rarely designed with non-traditional learners in mind
- Learners from non-English backgrounds are left behind — not because of lack of talent, but because of lack of access

**DATA247 was built specifically to close this gap.**

A structured, affordable, mobile-first preparation platform — available in both **English and Hinglish** — so that where you come from never determines how far you can go.

The Hinglish content in DATA247 is not a translation. It is a deliberate design decision — built for the millions of Indian learners who think, process, and learn best in a natural mix of Hindi and English. This is the everyday reality of a large majority of India's aspiring workforce — and it has been almost entirely ignored by existing preparation platforms.

DATA247 exists to change that.

## What DATA247 Does

DATA247 is a mobile-first learning platform that helps aspiring data professionals prepare for interviews, build skills, and enter the workforce with confidence.

The platform is designed for:
- Students from tier-2, tier-3 cities and rural India preparing for their first data role
- Learners from non-English or mixed-language backgrounds
- Working professionals switching into data analytics
- Anyone who needs structured, affordable, and practical preparation — regardless of where they studied or grew up

**Available in English and Hinglish** — making quality preparation genuinely accessible across India.

## How It Is Built

DATA247 is a mobile-first **Progressive Web App (PWA)** — published on **Google Play Store** as a Trusted Web Activity (TWA).

The platform runs on secure cloud infrastructure with real-time content management — meaning new study material, questions and notes can be added instantly by the founder without any app update required on the user's side.

**Infrastructure highlights:**
- Mobile-first — works on all devices and screen sizes
- Secure cloud database — users, questions, progress, flashcards
- Email and Google authentication
- Founder-only admin panel for real-time content updates
- Google Play Store distribution — package: app.data247.prep

## Why This Matters

India produces hundreds of thousands of data and analytics graduates every year. A significant portion of them struggle to convert their education into employment — not due to lack of talent, but due to lack of structured preparation access.

This problem is especially severe for learners outside major metros — those from tier-2 cities, tier-3 cities, and rural backgrounds — who do not have access to expensive coaching, English-medium support systems, or high-cost online platforms.

DATA247 is built for this majority.

A structured, affordable, mobile-first, bilingual preparation platform — designed with the Indian learner in mind — not just the learner who already has every advantage.
