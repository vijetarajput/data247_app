## The Story Behind DATA247

**Founded:** April 2026 &nbsp;&nbsp; **Launched:** June 2026 &nbsp;&nbsp; **Location:** Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India &nbsp;&nbsp; **Founder:** Vijetasingh Rajput

DATA247 started from a single observation:

> Many learners in India have access to information — but not access to structured preparation.

Structured data analytics preparation has largely been accessible to learners in tier-1 cities — those with access to premium coaching institutes, English-medium education, and high-cost online platforms.

However, a large and often overlooked segment of aspiring data professionals — learners from tier-2 cities, tier-3 cities, and rural India — face a very different reality:

- Quality preparation content is expensive and out of reach
- Most platforms assume full English fluency
- Coaching institutes charge fees that many cannot afford
- Structured learning paths are rarely designed with non-traditional learners in mind
- When learners get stuck, there is no affordable way to get expert help — the only options are expensive courses or unstructured internet searches

DATA247 was built specifically to close this gap.

A structured, affordable, mobile-first preparation platform — combined with an on-demand expert tutor marketplace — available in both English and Hinglish — so that where you come from never determines how far you can go.

The Hinglish content in DATA247 is not a translation. It is a deliberate design decision — built for the millions of Indian learners who think, process, and learn best in a natural mix of Hindi and English. This is the everyday reality of a large majority of India's aspiring workforce — and it has been almost entirely ignored by existing preparation platforms.

DATA247 exists to change that.

## What DATA247 Does

DATA247 operates as two complementary products:

**Mobile App** — A structured, gamified, daily preparation system for Data Analyst and Data Scientist roles. Available on Google Play Store.

**Web Platform** — A full browser-accessible version of all app features, plus an expert tutor marketplace where learners can book verified professionals for on-demand doubt clearing, interview preparation, resume review, and career guidance — starting at just ₹150 for 15 minutes.

Both products are designed for:

- Students from tier-2, tier-3 cities and rural India preparing for their first data role
- Learners from non-English or mixed-language backgrounds
- Working professionals switching into data analytics
- Anyone who needs structured, affordable, and practical preparation — regardless of where they studied or grew up

Available in English and Hinglish — making quality preparation genuinely accessible across India.

## Tutor Marketplace

The tutor marketplace was built in direct response to user feedback — mobile app users requested access to real experts, not just more content.

**Key features:**

- Any verified professional from any domain can register as a tutor
- LinkedIn PDF export used for profile verification — genuine credentials, no API required
- Sessions starting at ₹150 for 15 minutes — no monthly subscriptions, no long-term commitment
- Learners search by skill, company, university, or interview preparation role
- Booking-verified ratings and reviews — only learners who confirm a session can rate or review
- Unique shareable tutor URL — data247.co.in/t/[slug] — tutors can promote themselves anywhere
- Rate upgrade system — tutors unlock custom rates after 3 months with admin approval
- Zero platform commission during launch phase

## How It Is Built

DATA247 runs as two integrated products on secure cloud infrastructure.

| Component | Details |
|---|---|
| Mobile App | Progressive Web App (PWA) — published on Google Play Store via Trusted Web Activity (TWA) |
| Web Platform | React (TanStack Start) — hosted at data247.co.in |
| Backend and Database | Supabase (PostgreSQL) — Row Level Security enabled on all tables |
| Authentication | Email + Google OAuth — DPDP Act 2023 compliant |
| AI Features | Anthropic Claude — Resume AI Review, Salary Booster, LinkedIn PDF parsing |
| Security (Mobile) | External audit — MobSF v4.5.0 — no critical vulnerabilities |
| Security (Web) | RLS on all tables — server-side functions for restricted operations — PII protected via view layer |
| Compliance | DPDP Act 2023 compliant — Privacy Policy and Terms of Service live |
| Play Store | Package: app.data247.prep — Production submitted July 2026 |
